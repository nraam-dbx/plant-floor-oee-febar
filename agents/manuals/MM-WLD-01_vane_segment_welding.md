## Wire-feed degradation (failure code WIRE_FEED)
**Symptoms:** wire slipping, irregular arc, porosity in the weld bead, rising feeder vibration over 3–6 days.
**Root cause:** worn drive rolls and liner. **Repair MM-WLD-01-R2 (3 h):** LOTO per SOP-PLT-001; replace drive rolls
WF-ROLL-12 and liner WF-LNR-4M; re-tension feeder; weld a test coupon and X-ray before release.
**Prevention:** inspect drive rolls weekly; replace liner every 400 kg of wire.
